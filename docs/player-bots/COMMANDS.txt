1. GETTING STARTED
==================
Enter commands in Say chat. Names and IDs in angle brackets are placeholders;
replace them and omit the brackets. Use the lowercase command words shown.

Example: female human cleric
  #bot create Mercy 2 1 1
  #bot spawn Mercy
  #bot assist all
  #bot report Mercy

Bots belong to the CHARACTER that creates them, match its level, and use group
slots. Up to five bots can accompany one player, subject to available slots.
Names must be unique among characters and bots and contain 4-15 letters only.
All supported race/class combinations are allowed, even combinations unavailable
to normal player characters. Equipment race/class restrictions still apply.

Selecting a bot:
  #bot follow Mercy           Select by name.
  #bot follow target          Select your targeted, owned bot.
  #bot follow                 With no name, uses your targeted, owned bot.
  #bot follow byname Mercy    Explicit name selection.
  #bot follow all             Apply to your spawned bots.
  #bot follow spawned         Alias for all.
  #bot follow ownergroup      Alias for all in this build.

For commands with extra arguments, use a name or the literal target selector:
  #bot taunt target 1
For a spell directed at another creature, name the bot and target the recipient.
Omitting a spell target generally defaults to yourself, subject to spell rules.

Use all mainly for movement/combat commands. Many commands acting on spawned
bots also accept all, but a name is preferable for item transfers and settings.
Creation/list, saved parties, revival, deletion, inventories, supplies, and healing
rotation management have their own syntax below; do not assume all works there.
Most operational commands require the bot to be spawned. Roster commands,
inventory/supply listings, and certain rotation operations can use saved records.

2. COMPLETE COMMAND REFERENCE
=============================
Each line gives the command followed by its purpose or restrictions.

ROSTER AND LIFECYCLE
#bot
#bot help
  Show the built-in help. This document corrects its equipment slot note:
  equipment slots are 1-21; slot 0 is the player cursor, not a charm slot.
#bot list
  List bots belonging to your current character.
#bot create <Name> <ClassID> <RaceID> <GenderID>
  Create a saved bot. See the complete combination list at the end.
#bot spawn <Name>
  Spawn your saved bot into available group/raid-subgroup space.
#bot spawn all
  Try your roster in bot-ID order until the five-bot limit/free slots are reached.
#bot dismiss <Name|all>
#bot camp <Name|all>
  Save and dismiss. camp is an alias. Refused during combat.
#bot revive <Name>
  Revive an unspawned fallen bot after a 60-second wait, out of combat.
  Restores 1 HP and 0 mana; use spawn afterward. This is not player resurrection.
#bot delete <Name> confirm
  Permanently delete a saved bot. First dismiss it, retrieve equipment/supplies,
  and remove healing-rotation memberships. This is not a dismissal command.

MOVEMENT, COMBAT AND STATUS
#bot attack <Name|all>
  Attack your selected eligible NPC within 200 units of the bot.
#bot assist <Name|all>
  Enable combat assistance.
#bot passive <Name|all>
  Clear bot/pet combat targets, interrupt the bot's spell, and follow you.
#bot follow <Name|all>
  Follow you. Does not itself change the combat stance.
#bot stay <Name|all>
  Stop navigation and remain at the current spot; not a passive-combat toggle.
#bot summon <Name|all>
  Move the bot to you and resume following, only out of combat.
#bot report <Name|all>
  Display level, current/max HP, current/max mana, and pet name.
#bot suspend <Name|all>
  Suspend behavior, become passive, and stay. The bot remains spawned.
#bot release <Name|all>
  Clear suspension and resume assisting/following.
#bot ranged <Name> <0|1>
  Disable/enable ranged mode; usable weapon/ammunition and other checks apply.
#bot taunt <Name> <0|1>
  Disable/enable taunting.
#bot followdistance <Name> <Value>
  Set follow-distance parameter, 0-2500. Default stored value is 100.
  This is the server's internal distance parameter; do not assume feet/metres.

SPELLS AND UTILITY
These commands still check class, level, target, range, mana, reagents and reuse.
#bot spells <Name>
  List the bot's loaded spells.
#bot cast <Name> <SpellID>
  Cast a spell available to this bot's class and level on your selected target.
#bot cure <Name>
  Attempt an appropriate cure on the selected recipient.
#bot resurrect <Name>
#bot rez <Name>
  Attempt resurrection on a valid selected target. rez is an alias.
#bot mez <Name>
  Attempt mesmerization of the selected enemy.
#bot charm <Name>
  Attempt charm on the selected enemy.
#bot fear <Name>
  Attempt fear on the selected enemy.
#bot lull <Name>
  Attempt a lull spell on the selected enemy.
#bot invisibility <Name>
  Attempt invisibility on the selected recipient.
#bot invisundead <Name>
  Attempt invisibility versus undead on the selected recipient.
#bot levitate <Name>
  Attempt levitation on the selected recipient.
#bot waterbreathing <Name>
  Attempt water breathing on the selected recipient.
#bot departures <Name>
  List known group travel spells.
#bot depart <Name> <SpellID>
  Cast a known teleport/succor spell. Usual target and casting restrictions apply.
#bot evac <Name>
  Attempt an available group evacuation spell.
#bot songs <Name> <SpellID1> [SpellID2] [SpellID3] [SpellID4]
  Configure up to four bard songs known at the bot's level.
#bot songs <Name>
  Clear the saved bard song rotation.

PET COMMANDS
#bot pet <Name>
  Attempt to summon the bot's pet; class, spell and reagent checks apply.
#bot petdismiss <Name>
  Dismiss the pet out of combat and disable pet use. Charmed NPCs are released.
#bot petenabled <Name> <0|1>
  Disable/enable pet use; use 1 to re-enable after petdismiss.
  Bot pets are separate from your own pet. Supported summoned-pet state is saved.

SPELL SETTINGS AND HEALING PREFERENCES
#bot spellsettingsadd <Name> <SpellID> <Enabled> <MinHP> <MaxHP> <Priority>
#bot spellsettingsupdate <Name> <SpellID> <Enabled> <MinHP> <MaxHP> <Priority>
  Add/update a spell rule. Enabled: 0/1; HP bounds: 0-100 with MinHP <= MaxHP;
  priority: 0-1000, higher tried first. Use actual valid spell IDs.
#bot spellsettings <Name>
  List saved spell rules.
#bot spellsettingsdelete <Name> <SpellID>
  Remove a saved spell rule.
#bot enforcespellsettings <Name> <0|1>
  Disable/enable enforcement of the configured spell settings.
#bot blockedbuffs <Name> <SpellID> <0|1>
  1 blocks a valid beneficial spell on this bot; 0 unblocks it.
#bot healpercent <Name> <Percent>
  Set healing threshold, 0-100.
#bot announcecasts <Name> <0|1>
  Disable/enable casting announcements.

ABILITIES
#bot abilities <Name>
  List available class/level disciplines and enabled TAKP AAs.
#bot discipline <Name> <DisciplineID>
  Activate an available discipline. Use IDs from abilities.
#bot aa <Name> <AbilityID>
  Activate an available AA with class/level, state, target and reuse checks.
  AAs are NOT automatically granted by default. The package has an operator-only
  database option named autoaas, not a #bot autoaas command. Even when enabled,
  no AAs are granted below level 51. Unsupported AA actions are refused.

EQUIPMENT AND REAGENTS
#bot inventory <Name>
  List saved equipment slots, item names, item IDs and charges.
  Missing item definitions display Unknown item with the saved ID.
#bot equip <Name> <PlayerSlot> <BotSlot>
  Move an item from your general inventory slot 22-29 to bot equipment slot 1-21.
#bot unequip <Name> <EmptyPlayerSlot> <BotSlot>
  Return bot equipment to your empty general slot 22-29.
  IMPORTANT: PlayerSlot comes before BotSlot for BOTH commands.
#bot clickitem <Name> <BotSlot>
  Activate a supported equipped item click on your target (or you if untargeted).
#bot supply <Name> <PlayerSlot>
  Deposit a plain reagent stack from your general slot 22-29.
#bot supplies <Name>
  List deposited reagent item IDs and quantities.
#bot withdraw <Name> <EmptyPlayerSlot> <ItemID>
  Return up to one reagent stack to your empty general slot 22-29.

Direct trade is also supported: open a trade with your own bot and offer ordinary
equipment. Items are validated together; replaced gear and coins return to you.
Item transfers require no combat and valid ownership, item, lore, level, race,
class and weapon combinations. Bags/custom-data items are refused. Explicit
equip/unequip requires an empty destination. No-drop gear and stacked ammunition
are supported. Reagents are consumed when casting finishes.

HEALING ROTATIONS
Leader and Healer below are owned BOT NAMES; TargetName is a friendly group member.
Healing classes: cleric, paladin, ranger, druid, shaman, beastlord.
#bot healrotationcreate <Leader>
  Create a stopped rotation with the leader as its first healer.
#bot healrotationaddmember <Leader> <Healer>
  Add your healing bot. A healer can belong to only one rotation.
#bot healrotationremovemember <Leader> <Healer>
  Remove that healer from the rotation.
#bot healrotationaddtarget <Leader> <TargetName>
  Add a friendly group target. Spawn the leader and have the target present.
#bot healrotationremovetarget <Leader> <TargetName>
  Remove the named healing target.
#bot healrotationchangeinterval <Leader> <Milliseconds>
  Set interval from 500 to 60000 ms. Initial interval: 3000 ms.
#bot healrotationstart <Leader>
  Enable the rotation. Healers need to be spawned to act.
#bot healrotationstop <Leader>
  Disable the rotation while retaining its configuration.
#bot healrotationlist <Leader>
  List enabled state, interval, healers and targets.
#bot healrotationdelete <Leader>
  Remove the rotation configuration, not the bots.
Without explicit targets, the rotation selects injured group members.

SAVED PARTIES
#bot botgroupcreate <PartyName>
  Save your currently spawned bots. Reusing a party name replaces that selection.
#bot botgroupload <PartyName>
  Spawn the saved party into available slots. Dismiss other bots first if needed.
#bot botgrouplist
  List your saved parties and their bot members.
#bot botgroupdelete <PartyName>
  Delete the saved selection only; retain bots and their equipment.
Party names: 1-32 letters, numbers or underscores, no spaces.
Use the full command botgroupload, NOT the shorthand load from abbreviated help.

APPEARANCE
#bot appearance <Name> <Field> <Value>
  Fields: face, hair, haircolor, beard, beardcolor, eye1, eye2.
  Values must pass the race/gender-specific appearance checks (not every value
  works for every race). Dismiss and respawn to apply a saved appearance.
  This changes appearance choices, not the bot's race/class/gender.

There is no player #bot command in this package to rename a bot, change its
race/class/gender, set its level, or grant GM/account privileges. #companion is
a separate restricted development command and is not part of player bot usage.
This is not full modern EQEmu command parity; ^ commands from other servers
should not be assumed to work. Full client rendering and all class/level cases
were not exhaustively verified by the package's automated tests.

3. EQUIPMENT SLOTS
==================
PlayerSlot: 22-29 = eight top-level general inventory slots; not slots inside bags.
BotSlot:
  1 left ear       2 head          3 face          4 right ear
  5 neck           6 shoulders     7 arms          8 back
  9 left wrist    10 right wrist  11 ranged       12 hands
 13 primary       14 secondary    15 left finger  16 right finger
 17 chest         18 legs         19 feet         20 waist
 21 ammunition
Slot 0 is the cursor in TAKP, not a charm equipment slot.
Example: #bot equip Mercy 22 13
Example: #bot unequip Mercy 22 13   (your slot 22 must now be empty)

SIT AND MEDITATE (added 2026-10-05)
#bot sit Name on
#bot sit Name off
#bot sit all on
#bot sit all off
On forces currently spawned bots to sit and stay without following, assisting or casting until released. Owner movement and attacks do not release it. Off restores prior follow/stay mode and normal automatic resting. The response shows Meditate skill and current mana regeneration per tick. Forced sitting resets on dismiss/spawn, zoning and server restart.

DIRECT-DAMAGE CASTING TOGGLE
#bot dps cast off             Disable nukes for your currently spawned bots
#bot dps cast on              Enable nukes for your currently spawned bots
#bot dps cast off Lilly       Disable nukes only for Lilly
#bot dps cast on Lilly        Enable nukes only for Lilly
#bot report Lilly            Check the saved setting
Saved per bot. DoTs (including ones with an initial hit), buffs and heals stay
allowed. Instant lifetaps and damaging stuns are blocked while OFF. Existing
spell restrictions still apply. New bots default to ON.

RETREAT (added 2026-10-06)
#bot retreat Lilly on        Disengage Lilly and make her follow you
#bot retreat Lilly off       Release Lilly from retreat
#bot retreat all on          Retreat with all your currently spawned bots
#bot retreat all off         Release all your currently spawned bots
#bot report Lilly            Show whether retreat is enabled
Retreat interrupts current casting and stops attacks, taunt, offensive skills,
healing, buffs and other new casts. The bot and its pet follow instead of fighting,
even if you attack or target a different enemy. Assist and conflicting movement,
sit and ability commands cannot override retreat: turn it off explicitly first.
Turning retreat on releases forced sitting. Turning it off restores normal
behavior with the previous assist/passive setting; the bot keeps following unless
its saved suspend setting requires it to stay.
Saved per bot across zoning, dismissal, login and server restart; defaults to OFF.
The command applies only to your spawned bots. No database migration is required.
Retreat does not erase enemy hate, remove existing buffs/DoTs/damage shields,
grant immunity, or bypass roots, stuns, mesmerize, fear or normal pathfinding.
It does not evacuate or teleport the party.

BOT UPDATES (2026-10-12)
#bot revive Name             Revive a dead bot, then use #bot spawn Name
Revival has no timer. Owner must be alive and owner/bots/pets out of combat.
Revived bots return at full health and mana; normal spawn limits still apply.
#bot tankclose Name|all on|off   Move closer while respecting model clearance
#bot backattack Name|all on|off  Arrange attackers behind their enemy
#bot assist target PlayerOrBotName on|off
Select your bot first: assist target affects only that bot and overrides normal
assist until disabled. The source must share your group/raid or be the owner.
Retreat retains priority. See the detailed command documents for restrictions.
