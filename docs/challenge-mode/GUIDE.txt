CHALLENGE MODE
Installed October 8, 2026

PLAYER COMMANDS
#challenge mode on
#challenge mode status

Normal activation requires level 1, being outside Plane of Knowledge,
and having no saved bind point in Plane of Knowledge. The setting belongs
to this character and persists through zoning, logout and server restarts.
The normal command cannot disable Challenge Mode.

RULES
PoK books cannot be used before level 45. Clicking one explains the limit.
This blocks books, not every possible travel route into PoK.

Bots can be created at any level. Concurrent summoned bot limits:
Levels 1-4:   0
Levels 5-14:  1
Levels 15-24: 2
Levels 25-34: 3
Levels 35-44: 4
Level 45+:   5 in a normal group; additional bots in an existing raid.

At 45, the challenge level cap is removed. Normal group/raid capacity still
applies: six per group, 72 total raid members including players. The raid
leader can fill additional raid groups. Losing levels can dismiss excess
bots. Changing mode also enforces the resulting capacity.

Death XP loss and equipment/cash-bearing corpses apply starting at level 1.
Recover carried equipment, items and money from your corpse. Bank contents
remain in the bank. Normal soulbound and death-exception rules still apply.

No existing character was enrolled automatically.
